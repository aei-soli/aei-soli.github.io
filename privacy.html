Privacy Policy

Applies to: SOLI Solitaire (Android, iOS, macOS, Windows & Web)  ·  Effective date: 18 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 18 June 2026

Short version: SOLI stores your game progress and statistics on your own device. Your chosen display name (an alias) and score are sent to a public leaderboard only if you choose to enable it. The free, ad-supported version shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. A one-time SOLI Premium purchase removes all ads. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Advertising Free version

The free version of SOLI on mobile (Android and iOS) displays advertisements through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, AdMob accesses your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and limited ad-interaction and diagnostic data. Ads may be personalised based on this identifier.

  • You can purchase SOLI Premium (a one-time, non-consumable purchase) to remove all advertising permanently.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, where required, SOLI presents a consent request before serving personalised ads, and you may choose non-personalised ads.
  • iOS users are asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and AdMob's policies are at support.google.com/admob.

4. In-App Purchases Optional

SOLI offers a one-time Premium upgrade that removes ads and unlocks all table themes and card styles. Purchases are processed by the platform's billing system — Google Play Billing on Android, Apple In-App Purchase on iOS, or Stripe on web, macOS, and Windows — and validated through RevenueCat, Inc. We never receive or store your full payment-card details; those are handled entirely by the payment processor.

To restore a purchase, you may be asked to provide an email address; it is used only to locate and restore your entitlement.

5. The Global Leaderboard Optional

SOLI includes an optional global leaderboard powered by Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC, United States). This feature is disabled by default.

If you choose to enable the leaderboard (at first launch or later via the in-app Privacy screen), SOLI transmits your chosen display name and your score to Firebase when you record a qualifying result. No other data is transmitted.

  • Your display name is chosen by you — it does not need to be your real name.
  • Submitted scores are publicly visible to all players on the leaderboard.
  • You can disable leaderboard submission at any time via the in-app Privacy screen.
  • To request deletion of previously submitted scores, contact us at the address above.

Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Local Data Storage

Your scores, statistics, preferences, and consent choice are stored locally on your device (for example, in the app's private storage on Android/iOS, or in ~/Library/Application Support/SOLI/ on macOS). You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application. No copy of this local data is held by AEI Digital Solutions.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

AEI Digital Solutions is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as a data controller. Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — leaderboard display-name/score transmission and personalised advertising. You provide explicit consent and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — processing a purchase you have requested.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — local storage of game data and basic, non-personalised ad delivery, security, and fraud prevention.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, via the in-app Privacy screen

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@aei-digitalsolutions.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Retention

  • Local data — retained on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application.
  • Leaderboard data — retained in Firebase until you request deletion.
  • Advertising & purchase data — retained by Google, Apple, Stripe, and RevenueCat according to their own retention policies.
