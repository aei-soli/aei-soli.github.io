Privacy Policy

Short version: SOLI stores your game statistics only on your own Mac. Your name and score are only sent to a public leaderboard if you explicitly choose to enable it. We do not collect your email address, location, device ID, or any personal data beyond what you voluntarily provide.

3. The Global Leaderboard Optional

SOLI includes an optional global leaderboard powered by Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC, United States). This feature is disabled by default.

If you choose to enable the leaderboard at first launch (or later via Menu → Privacy), SOLI will transmit your chosen display name and your score to Firebase when you complete a Daily Challenge. No other data is transmitted.

  • Your display name is chosen by you — it does not need to be your real name.
  • Submitted scores are publicly visible to all players on the leaderboard.
  • You can disable leaderboard submission at any time via Menu → Privacy → Play Privately.
  • To request deletion of previously submitted scores, contact us at the address above.

Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Local Data Storage

All game data is stored in a folder on your Mac at:

~/Library/Application Support/SOLI/

This folder contains your scores, statistics, preferences, and consent choice. You can delete this folder at any time to reset the application completely. No copy of this data is held by AEI Digital Solutions.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

AEI Digital Solutions is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as a data controller. Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — leaderboard name and score transmission. You provide explicit consent at first launch and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — local storage of game statistics and preferences, necessary to provide the core application functionality.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, via Menu → Privacy inside the app

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@aei-digitalsolutions.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data — retained on your Mac until you delete the app's support folder or uninstall the application.
  • Leaderboard data — retained in Firebase until you request deletion by contacting us.
