Prismeal — Privacy Policy

Applies to: Prismeal (iOS, Android & Web)  ·  Effective date: [set at launch]

Short version: Prismeal stores the meals you log — photos or descriptions — together with your goal and a few health-related inputs, so it can analyse your food and coach you. This is sensitive (health) data, processed only with your explicit consent. Your full history is yours: you can export everything or delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time. We do not sell your personal data, and we show no advertising.

1. Who We Are

AEI Digital Solutions is an independent software studio operated by Adrian Eugen Ionescu. We develop and distribute Prismeal for iOS, Android, and the web.

Data controller: AEI Digital Solutions
Contact: contact@aei-digitalsolutions.com

3. Sensitive (Health) Data & Your Consent

Your meals, symptoms, weight and goals are health-related "special category" data under Article 9 of the GDPR. Prismeal processes them only on your explicit consent, which you give during onboarding and can withdraw at any time by deleting your data or your account (Section 7). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To identify foods and estimate nutrition from your photos or descriptions.
  • To calculate your Metabolic Integrity Score and provide goal-aware coaching, check-ins and progress.
  • To sync your data across your devices and operate your subscription.
  • To keep the service secure and comply with the law.

5. AI Processing

To analyse a meal, the photo or description you submit is sent to Google's Gemini API, which returns the structured analysis. This processing is carried out to provide the service you requested, and is governed by Google's API terms.

6. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

  • Explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 9(2)(a)) — for your meal, symptom, weight and goal (health) data.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the app and manage your subscription.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, abuse prevention, and basic operation.

7. Your Data, In Your Control

You can exercise the main GDPR rights directly inside the app, at any time:

  • Export — Settings → Export my data downloads everything (meals, follow-ups, profile, KPIs) in a machine-readable file (portability).
  • Delete — Settings → Delete my account & data permanently removes your account and all associated data from our live systems (erasure).

You may also access, rectify, restrict, object to processing, and withdraw consent, by using the app or emailing contact@aei-digitalsolutions.com. We respond within one month.

9. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our live systems; residual copies in backups are removed on the normal backup cycle. We may retain minimal records where required by law.

10. Security

Data is stored in access-controlled, user-scoped databases — each user can reach only their own data — photos are size- and type-restricted, our AI key is held server-side only, and transport is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

11. Children's Privacy

Prismeal is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 to create an account (the age of digital consent in Romania), and weight-management coaching is offered only to users 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always at this URL, with an updated effective date, and we will ask for renewed consent where the law requires it.
