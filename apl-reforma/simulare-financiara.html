How the proposed model works

The steps follow the workbook in order. The figures in them follow your settings. Words in the glossary at the bottom of the page are explained there.

  1. Taxes collected in 2025On each unit's territory the State Tax Service collected personal income tax (PIT, 9,867.9 M lei outside UTAG), corporate income tax (CIT, 9,547.2 M lei) and VAT (14,985.4 M lei). The model shares parts of these three taxes.
  2. Direct sharesEach unit keeps part of the PIT and CIT collected on its own territory: LPA1 units 50% of PIT and 0% of CIT; the three regions 0% / 0% of what their LPA1 units collect; Chișinău, Bălți and Taraclia 50% / 0%. Together: 4,933.9 M lei.
  3. PoolsWhat the units do not keep forms a PIT pool (4,933.9 M lei) and a CIT pool (9,547.2 M lei). VAT goes into a pool whole (14,985.4 M lei).
  4. Two equalisation fundsGTF1, for LPA1 units and Taraclia, takes 27% of the PIT pool, 0% of the CIT pool and 9% of VAT: 2,680.8 M lei. GTF2 is not used: level II gets a fixed envelope instead (809.0 M lei, the regions' 2025 general money after the culture transfer), paid from the state budget: operating grants for the three regions (67.5 M lei), task money for Chișinău, Bălți and Taraclia (25% of the rest, 185.4 M lei, by population) and the regions' development fund (556.1 M lei, by population). The rest of each tax stays with the state budget.
  5. Incentive grant2% of GTF1 (53.6 M lei) is set aside for LPA1 units that collect more own revenue (local taxes and the property tax) relative to their PIT than 1.00× the average. A first round shares it by effort; a second round shares what is left by each unit's room under its cap of 20% of own revenues, so no unit goes above the cap. 45.6 M lei is paid to 112 units; the 8.0 M lei the caps leave unpaid stays in the LPA1 fund.
  6. EqualisationThe rest of GTF1 (2,635.2 M lei) is split between the 245 LPA1 units and Taraclia by a key: 65% their share of the population, 20% their share of the area, 15% their share of the fiscal-capacity gap, meaning how far their PIT per head falls below 1.30× the average, times their population (so the gap counts per inhabitant, not per unit). The three regions get their share of the fixed envelope, for now their own 2025 baseline after the culture transfer. Taraclia also keeps its PIT share; Chișinău and Bălți get their direct shares only.
  7. New totalFor each unit: direct shares + equalisation + incentive grant. Outside UTAG: 8,423.8 M lei.
  8. Compared with 2025The 2025 baseline is what each unit actually received in 2025 under today's law: its shares of state taxes, its equalisation transfer and any compensation transfer, with the culture institutions that move from the regions to level I units (215.8 M lei) taken off the regions' baseline and added to the towns that take them over (8,418.0 M lei in all). A unit gains if its new total is above its baseline.
  9. Phase-inEvery unit outside UTAG moves to the model over 4 years: in year 1 it gets its baseline plus 25 in a hundred of its change, then one more step each year. In year 1 the 97 units that lose get top-ups of 327.7 M lei from the state budget, and the units that gain wait for 332.0 M lei of their gains: net −4.4 M lei for the state budget.

What stays as it is

These amounts are the same under the proposed model and in 2025, so they do not change who gains or loses. They are left out of both totals and shown here for scale (all outside UTAG):

  • Road tax 2025, 914.4 M lei (datasheet W): the share of the road fund paid to local budgets, as executed in 2025.
  • Other general transfers under the 2025 budget law (LP293), 150.3 M lei (datasheet X).
  • Earmarked transfers, 17,510.6 M lei (datasheet T), mostly for education; they fund specific services and are outside both systems.
  • Own revenues of the LPA1 units (local taxes and the property tax), 738.0 M lei: each unit keeps them in both systems. The model uses them only to measure fiscal effort for the incentive grant.
  • Education (datasheet Y) would be taken out of the baseline; the column is empty in the workbook (0.0 M lei).
  • UTAG (Gagauzia) keeps its own arrangement and is outside the model, the comparison and the totals.

The 2025 compensation transfers (47.4 M lei, datasheet V) are part of the baseline, as money the units received in 2025. So are the culture institutions moved from the regions to level I units (datasheet Z, sheet Culture 2025): 215.8 M lei added to the towns that take them over and taken off the regions' baseline.

Glossary

LPA1
First-level local authorities: villages, communes and towns (primării). On the new map, 245 units outside UTAG.
LPA2
Second-level authorities: the three regions (Nord, Centru, Sud) and the municipalities Chișinău, Bălți and Taraclia.
PIT, CIT, VAT
Personal income tax, corporate income tax, value added tax. Amounts are what was collected on each unit's territory in 2025 (the State Tax Service's contingents).
GTF1, GTF2
The two equalisation funds (general transfer funds) of the proposed model: GTF1 for LPA1 units and Taraclia, GTF2 for the regions (not used: the regions get a fixed envelope).
Equalisation key
Each unit's share of a fund, a weighted mix of its shares of population, area and (for LPA1) the fiscal-capacity gap.
Fiscal capacity
PIT collected per inhabitant. The gap is how far a unit is below the multiplier × the LPA1 average; units above it have no gap. A unit's share of the capacity part is its gap × its population, over the sum of gap × population.
Baseline (AA)
What each unit received in 2025 under today's law, summed to the new map: tax shares + equalisation transfer + compensation transfers − education + culture institutions moved (Z).
Phase-in
The transition of the new model concept: each unit gets its baseline plus one step of its change per year until the full model. CA is the year-1 adjustment: a top-up for units that lose, a part of the gain held back for units that gain (dashboard F49, F52).
Amount in year 1
What a unit receives in the first year: the new model + the year-1 adjustment (workbook column CB).
UTAG
The autonomous territorial unit of Gagauzia, outside the model.
Workbook
The team's Excel model (09.10.2026 UPDATED_ LPF model_2025 data and TA map v8.xlsx). This page repeats its formulas; it does not read or change the file.
Default settings
The parameter values as they are saved in the workbook, and the results they give. Columns marked “default settings” show the workbook's own figures; “your settings vs default” shows how far your changes move a unit (– means no change).
Cell references
C16, F46 and so on are cells of the workbook's dashboard sheet; AL, BN, AA are columns of its datasheet sheet.
